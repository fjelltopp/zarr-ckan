Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Welcome to the Zambia National Research Repository (ZNRR) FAQ page. Below you will find answers to common questions about using the platform, accessing research, submitting data, and managing your account.

Getting Started

What is the Zambia National Research Repository (ZNRR)?

The ZNRR is a centralized digital platform that collects, preserves, and disseminates research outputs, evaluation studies, and data relevant to Zambia's development. It is managed by the Ministry of Finance and National Planning in collaboration with Smart Zambia Institute.

Do I need an account to access research materials?

No. All research materials on the ZNRR are openly accessible to the public. You can browse, search, and download datasets and documents without creating an account. However, an account is required if you wish to submit research or become a contributor.

How do I create an account?

To create an account, click the "Login" button at the top right of the homepage, then select "Register". You will need to provide your name, email address, and create a password. Accounts are typically approved within 1-2 business days.

Searching and Accessing Data

How do I search for research on the platform?

You can use the search bar on the homepage to search by keywords, title, author, or organization. Advanced filters allow you to narrow results by publication year, format, license type, and subject area.

What file formats are supported for download?

The ZNRR supports a wide range of formats including PDF (documents), CSV, Excel (datasets), as well as audio, video, and image files. All downloadable materials are clearly labeled with their format and file size.

Can I preview a dataset before downloading?

Yes. For many datasets, you can preview the data directly in your browser before downloading. This feature is available for CSV, Excel, and other tabular data formats.

Submitting Research

Who can submit research to the ZNRR?

Researchers affiliated with recognized academic institutions, government agencies, research organizations, and NGOs can submit research. You must have an approved account and be associated with an organization that is registered on the platform.

What types of research can I submit?

We accept research papers, policy briefs, evaluation reports, datasets, theses, dissertations, and other research outputs related to Zambia's development. All submissions undergo a review process before publication.

How long does the review process take?

The review process typically takes 5-7 business days. You will receive email notifications as your submission moves through the review workflow.

Do I retain copyright over my submitted work?

Yes. You retain full copyright over your work. By submitting to ZNRR, you grant the repository a non-exclusive license to preserve and distribute your work. You can find full details in our Copyright Policy.

Account Management

How do I reset my password?

Click "Login" and then "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email address, and you will receive a link to reset your password. If you need assistance, contact support at repository@grz.gov.zm.

How do I update my profile information?

Log into your account, go to "My Account", then select "View/Edit Profile". You can update your name, email, organization affiliation, and other details.

How do I manage datasets I have submitted?

Log into your account, go to "My Account", then select "My Data". From here you can edit, update, or deprecate your submitted datasets.

Technical Support

What browser should I use?

We recommend using the latest versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari for the best experience.

What should I do if I encounter an error?

Please note the error message and contact our support team at repository@grz.gov.zm with a description of the issue and any screenshots if possible.

How can I request new features or report bugs?

You can submit feedback through the "Contact" page or email us directly at repository@grz.gov.zm. We value your input and continuously work to improve the platform.

Policies and Guidelines

What are the data protection requirements?

All submissions must comply with the Data Protection Act, 2021. Do not upload any personally identifiable information (PII) without consent. Sensitive data should be anonymized before submission.

Are there restrictions on who can access certain data?

Most data is openly accessible. However, some datasets may have restricted access due to licensing agreements or sensitivity. These will be clearly marked with access conditions.

How is the ZNRR funded?

The ZNRR is funded by the Government of the Republic of Zambia through the Ministry of Finance and National Planning, with technical support from Smart Zambia Institute.

Still have questions?

If you cannot find the answer to your question here, please contact us at repository@grz.gov.zm.
